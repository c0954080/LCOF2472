Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What materials are used for the housing sheds?
A: Our sheds are constructed from durable, weather-resistant materials such as treated wood, galvanized steel, and high-quality vinyl. Each material is chosen for its longevity and ability to withstand the elements.
Q: Can I customize the design of my shed?
A: Absolutely! We offer a range of customization options, including size, color, roofing style, and more. Simply contact us with your requirements, and we will design a shed to suit your needs.
Q: How long will it take to build my shed?
A: Construction times vary based on the size and customization of your shed. On average, it takes 2-4 weeks from order to completion. We will provide you with a specific timeline once your order is confirmed.
Q: What is your return policy?
A: We offer a 30-day return policy on all unused cutting boards. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, please contact us to arrange a return or exchange.
Q: Do you offer delivery?
A: Yes, we offer delivery services for all our sheds. Delivery times depend on your location, but we work with reliable carriers to ensure timely delivery to your doorstep..
Q: What is your return policy?
A: We offer a 30-day return policy on all standard, unused sheds. If you're not completely satisfied with your shed, you can request a return or exchange. Custom-built sheds are non-returnable unless there is a manufacturing defect.
Q: How do I maintain my shed?
A: To keep your shed in great condition, we recommend regular cleaning and occasional maintenance, such as checking for any damage and applying protective coatings to wood surfaces to prevent rot.
Q: Do you offer warranties on your sheds?
A: Yes, all of our sheds come with a warranty. The length and terms of the warranty vary depending on the materials used, but typically we offer a 5-10 year warranty for the structure and materials.
Q: Can I build the shed myself, or do you provide installation?
A: We provide both options. You can opt to have our team install the shed for you, or you can choose a DIY kit with instructions for assembly. If you prefer professional installation, we will schedule it as soon as your shed is ready.
